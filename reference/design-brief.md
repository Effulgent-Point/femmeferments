# Femme Ferments - Design Brief

## Client Feedback
- Current site is "too white"
- The pale purple "makes us want to vomit"
- Want a whole new style and design
- Inspired by the bottle labels and event flyer aesthetic

## Design Direction
- Rich, vibrant jewel tones from the broken glass mosaic art
- NOT pale/pastel — bold, saturated colors
- "Beauty in the Broken Glass" as central design metaphor
- Empowerment, community, resilience themes
- Wine-forward but mission-driven
- Nonprofit feel without looking cheap

## Color Palette Inspiration (from bottle labels)
- Deep teal/emerald greens
- Sapphire/cobalt blues
- Rich ruby/crimson reds
- Warm amber/gold
- Deep purple/amethyst
- Dark backgrounds (the dark bottle is striking)
- Pops of bright color against dark

## Typography Direction
- "FEMME FERMENTS" on bottles: clean, modern, spaced caps
- "Beauty in the Broken Glass" on bottles: elegant cursive/script
- Flyer: mix of elegant serif headers with clean body text

## Key Pages Needed
1. Home - hero, mission, wines preview, events, community CTA
2. The Cause - mission deep-dive, partner orgs, impact
3. The Wines - wine cards with descriptions, purchase info
4. Events - upcoming events, past events gallery
5. Contact - form, location, social links
6. About - story, team, values

## Footer Requirement
- "Made with heart by Effulgent Point" (link to https://effulgentpoint.com/)

## Hosting Strategy
- Custom CMS (adapted from soccersite pattern)
- Next.js static export + JSON content store + PHP API
- Hosted on GoDaddy
- Built-in admin panel for client to edit content
- More flexible than soccersite version (richer content types, better theming)

## Similar Sites Researched
- Women's Wine Alliance (womenswinealliance.com) - professional, community-focused
- Della Donna Wine (delladonnawine.com) - elegant, minimalist, celebration of women
- Women of Wine (women-of-wine.org) - professional symposium style
- Batonnage - global women in wine nonprofit network
- Wine Empowered - tuition-free wine education for women/minorities

## Nonprofit Design Inspiration
- Malala Fund - beautiful, feminine but empowered color scheme
- Brown Girls Doc Mafia - strong imagery, sleek design
- Ocean Conservancy - emotional imagery, clean professional
- Bold typography, story-driven layouts, donation CTAs

The Vision

Beauty Begins Where Things Break

Femme Ferments started with a simple belief: that broken things — glass, circumstance, chance — can be gathered back together and made beautiful. Every bottle we craft carries that belief forward, one vintage at a time.

100% of our profits empower women.

The Land

Rooted in the Willamette Valley

Our wines are grown and crafted in the heart of Oregon’s Willamette Valley, among the rolling hills and volcanic soils that make this one of the world’s most storied wine regions. Every grape we work with tells the story of this place.

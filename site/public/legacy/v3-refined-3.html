Chapter One

Beauty Begins Where Things Break

Femme Ferments began with a simple, stubborn idea: that wine — an industry long shaped by men's names on the label — could instead become a vessel for women's independence. We gather donated fruit, borrowed cellars, and volunteer hands to craft small-batch wines, and we funnel every dollar of profit back into programs that give women the resources to build something of their own.

“100% of our profits empower women.”

Chapter Two

Rooted in the Willamette Valley

Willamette Valley's cool nights and volcanic soil have made it one of the great Pinot Noir regions on earth — but the vineyards and cellars here have opened their gates to us for another reason too. Partner growers set aside rows of fruit each harvest, and neighboring wineries lend us press time and tank space, because they believe the valley is big enough to grow both great wine and greater opportunity.

No. 01

The Harvester

What You'll Do
  • Pick and sort donated fruit at partner vineyards during harvest weekends
  • Work alongside other volunteers under the guidance of our vineyard partners
  • Help transport fruit from the vine to the partner cellar
What We Provide
  • Gloves, buckets, and all harvesting tools
  • Snacks, water, and shade breaks throughout the day
  • A Femme Ferments harvest t-shirt for your first shift

A few weekend mornings each September and October.

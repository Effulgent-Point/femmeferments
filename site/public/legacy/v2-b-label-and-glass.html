Written in the Willamette Valley

Every wine label tells you what’s inside the bottle. Ours tells you where the bottle came from — and who it’s for.

Femme Ferments began the way most good things do: with more broken pieces than anyone knew what to do with. A market that didn’t want certain lots of fruit. A community of women winemakers with more skill than opportunity. A valley full of growers, cellars, and volunteers who all believed something valuable could still be made from what was left over.

So we started gathering the pieces.

“We didn’t set out to make wine without breaking a few things first. We set out to prove the broken things were worth keeping.”

Every vintage we bottle is assembled the same way a mosaic is assembled — not smoothed over, not disguised, but arranged so the cracks catch the light. Donated grapes. Borrowed cellar time. Saturday mornings spent bottling by hand. None of it looks like a traditional winery, and that is rather the point.

One hundred percent of what we earn moves directly into programs that support women across Oregon — no exceptions, no overhead skimmed off the top. What starts broken in the vineyard ends up, we hope, a little more whole in someone’s life.

Your Part

Six Ways to Pour Yourself In

Femme Ferments only works because a whole community pours itself in. Here is where you fit.

No. 01

The Harvester

Pick donated fruit at partner vineyards each harvest — no experience necessary, just willing hands.

No. 02

The Storyteller

Photograph the crush, write the newsletter, keep the community in the loop between vintages.

No. 03

The Host

Organize tastings and gatherings that introduce new people to what we’re building.

No. 04

The Builder

Lend a hand with the website, the logistics, the spreadsheets nobody else wants to touch.

No. 05

The Connector

Introduce us to growers, suppliers, and donors who share the mission.

No. 06

The Maker

Join us in the cellar for bottling, labeling, and corking days.

Our Origin

Beauty Begins Where Things Break

Femme Ferments began with a broken bottle, a borrowed corner of a cellar, and a stubborn belief that something beautiful could come from what most people discard. We collect donated grapes, borrowed equipment, and volunteer hours that would otherwise go unused, and turn them into wine worth celebrating. Every label, every pour, and every dollar raised carries the same conviction — that beauty isn't found despite the broken pieces. It's found because of them.

“100% of our profits empower women.”

Our Home

Rooted in the Willamette Valley

Our wine grows in the same volcanic and sedimentary soils that make this valley one of the most celebrated Pinot Noir regions in the world. But our roots run deeper than terroir. Every partner vineyard, every cellar that opens its doors, and every winemaker who lends an afternoon is part of a growing network of Oregonians who believe wine can do more than taste good — it can do good.

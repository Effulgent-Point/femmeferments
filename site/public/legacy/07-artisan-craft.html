Wines Crafted for a Purpose

Printed in the Willamette Valley — Est. this Vintage

Every bottle we produce begins with a broken thing made beautiful again. Femme Ferments was founded on the belief that craft — patient, deliberate, hand-tended craft — can carry more than flavor. It can carry purpose.

We partner with small growers and women-led vineyard crews across the Valley, harvesting fruit by hand and fermenting in small lots. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is mass-produced. Each label is numbered, each vintage limited.

And when the glass is empty, the work continues: one hundred percent of our profits are donated directly to organizations that empower women across Oregon — because a bottle shared should leave the world a little better than it found it.

From the Valley

Grown in Willamette, Made by Hand

The Willamette Valley's cool nights and volcanic, wind-blown soils have long drawn winemakers seeking something honest. We work these same rows — hand-pruned, hand-picked, fermented in small open-top lots — alongside the women-owned vineyards who share our patience for the process.

Nothing here is engineered for scale. It's grown for character, and bottled for a cause.

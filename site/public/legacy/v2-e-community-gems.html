Who We Are

This is bigger than wine.

“We're a community of volunteers, dreamers, and winemakers who believe every woman deserves a seat at the table.”

The wine is what brings us together — but the reason we keep showing up is each other. Every bottle represents dozens of hands, hundreds of volunteer hours, and one shared belief: our community shows up for one another, every single time.

Chapter One

The Vision

A Nonprofit Wine Collective · Willamette Valley, Oregon
What if every bottle told a story of resilience?

Femme Ferments began with a question, not a business plan. We looked at the broken glass scattered across a studio floor and saw something worth keeping — proof that things which shatter can still catch the light. That idea became a wine label, and the label became a promise: every bottle we release exists to prove that beauty and brokenness are not opposites.

We are a nonprofit wine collective rooted in Oregon’s Willamette Valley, built by volunteers, donated fruit, and borrowed cellar space. We don’t sell wine to turn a profit — we make wine because it lets us give one away. One hundred percent of what we earn goes toward empowering women, one small-batch vintage at a time.

II
Chapter Two

The Land

Volcanic Soil · Marine Sediment · Patient Growers

Our story begins in the volcanic soil of Oregon’s Willamette Valley — a place where cool nights and generations of patient growers coax extraordinary character out of every cluster. We don’t own vineyards. We partner with them. Small, family-run sites across the Eola-Amity Hills open their rows to us each season, donating fruit that would otherwise go to market, because they believe a valley this generous should give back in more ways than wine alone.

Rooted in soil, sustained by generosity.

The Mission

0%
Of Our Profits Empower Women

Almost everything we use is given, not bought. Tap each step to see how a donated grape becomes lasting impact.

Donated
Grapes
Partner vineyards across the Eola-Amity Hills donate their fruit each harvest — no purchase orders, just growers who believe in the mission.
Volunteer
Harvest
A rotating crew of volunteers picks, sorts, and presses every cluster by hand — punching down and racking through the whole ferment.
Partner
Cellar
Wine is finished, aged, and bottled in borrowed cellar space — a partner winery lending equipment and expertise, vintage after vintage.
Donated
Corks
Valley suppliers donate corks and closures — keeping our costs, and our footprint, as small as our ambitions are big.
Crafted Wine
100% Impact
Every finished bottle sold sends its full profit to nonprofits that empower women in our community — no exceptions.

Chapter Five

Our Community, Piece by Piece

Every Piece Makes the Picture

Whatever time, skill, or connection you bring, there’s a place for it in this story.

The Volunteer

Pick grapes at partner vineyards — no experience necessary, just willing hands.

The Winemaker

Lend your expertise in the cellar — from crush to bottling, we welcome the guidance.

The Dreamer

Photography, design, and storytelling that carry our story forward, bottle by bottle.

The Organizer

Help plan tastings and launch parties that bring the community together, glass in hand.

The Advocate

Spread the word and connect us with nonprofits doing the work. Every introduction plants a seed.

The Partner

Donate grapes, equipment, or cellar space — the backbone of every vintage we release.

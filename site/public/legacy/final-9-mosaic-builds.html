Chapter One

The Vision

What if every bottle told a story of resilience?

Femme Ferments began with a shared bottle of wine and a promise: that winemaking could be an act of collective generosity, not just a product to sell. A handful of women in the Willamette Valley wanted the profit from every bottle to matter more than the bottle itself — so they built a nonprofit collective around that single idea.

No investors, no owners. Just neighbors who believed a glass of wine could carry something bigger than a harvest.

02
Chapter Two

The Land

Rooted in the Willamette Valley.

Our wine grows from the volcanic soil and marine sediment of Oregon’s Eola-Amity Hills — a place where cool nights and patient growers coax extraordinary character from every cluster. We don’t own vineyards; we partner with them. Small, family-run sites open their rows to us each season, donating fruit that would otherwise go to market.

03
Chapter Three

The Craft

Made by many hands.

Every bottle is assembled from a community’s generosity: donated grapes, volunteers who show up for harvest, a borrowed cellar we’re lent vintage after vintage, and corks donated by valley suppliers. What we lack in budget, we make up for in the number of hands willing to help.

Chapter Four

The Mission

0%

Of our profits empower women.

Because every step of the process is donated — the grapes, the labor, the cellar, the corks — there’s almost nothing left to spend our profit on except our mission. Every dollar earned from a bottle of Femme Ferments goes directly toward programs and grants that empower women in our community.

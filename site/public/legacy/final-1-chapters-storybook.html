II
Chapter Two

The Land

Rooted in the Willamette Valley.

Our story begins in the volcanic soil and marine sediment of Oregon’s Willamette Valley — a place where cool nights and patient growers coax extraordinary character from every cluster. We don’t own vineyards. We partner with them. Small, family-run sites across the Eola-Amity Hills open their rows to us each season, donating fruit that would otherwise go to market, because they believe a valley this generous should give back in more ways than wine alone.

Chapter Three

The Craft

Made by many hands.

Nothing about our wine is manufactured in the ordinary sense. Every bottle is assembled from an entire community’s generosity: donated grapes, a rotating crew of volunteers who show up for the harvest, borrowed cellar space we’re lent vintage after vintage, and corks donated by valley suppliers who’d rather see them poured than sit on a shelf. What we lack in budget, we make up for in the number of hands willing to help.

IV
Chapter Four

The Mission

0%
Of Our Profits Empower Women

It works because almost everything we use is given, not bought. Tap each step below to see how a donated grape becomes real, lasting impact.

Partner vineyards in Eola-Amity Hills donate their fruit each harvest season — no purchase orders, just growers who believe in the mission.
A rotating crew of volunteers picks, sorts, and presses every cluster by hand — punching down and racking through the whole ferment.
Wine is finished, aged, and bottled in a borrowed cellar — our partner winery lending space and equipment, vintage after vintage.
Supporting nonprofits that empower women in our community — every dollar of profit, no exceptions.

Our Mission

Every bottle poured is a bridge to possibility. Femme Ferments crafts wine in the heart of Oregon's Willamette Valley with a singular purpose: every dollar of profit funds programs that help women rebuild, rise, and flourish. This isn't charity wine — it's wine with a mission fermenting inside every drop.

Our Story

Rooted in Oregon's Willamette Valley, Femme Ferments began with women who understood that renewal is possible — even from what's broken. Rolling hills, volcanic soil, and cool maritime air create a place where fragile things, grapes and people alike, are given room to become extraordinary.

Each label is hand-painted in watercolor, a mosaic pieced from shards of glass — a reminder that what shatters can be remade into something luminous.

The Broken Glass

Our labels are original watercolor mosaics, each one built from fragments — a visual promise that breaking is never the end of the story.

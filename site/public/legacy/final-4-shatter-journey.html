Our Mission
0%
Of Our Profits Empower Women

Femme Ferments is a nonprofit wine collective in Oregon’s Willamette Valley. We don’t sell wine to make a profit — we make wine because it lets us give one away. Every bottle exists because someone said yes: a grower who donated fruit, a volunteer who gave a Saturday, a winery that opened its cellar door. That chain of generosity is the whole reason 100% of our profit can go straight to empowering women.

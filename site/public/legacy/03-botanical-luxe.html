Our Story

Rooted in the Valley

Femme Ferments began with a simple idea: that wine, like community, is stronger when it's built on partnership. We work hand-in-hand with growers across the Willamette Valley, tending small parcels of fruit with the same care they've given this land for generations.

Our first release grows in the Eola-Amity Hills, where volcanic soil and marine sediment meet cool Pacific winds — a terroir that produces wines of uncommon poise. It's a place where things that seem fragile turn out to be extraordinarily resilient. Much like the women we exist to support.

Every decision, from the vine to the label, is made in service of craftsmanship, integrity, and community — because a wine crafted with purpose should taste like it.

Strength in the fractures. Grace in the pieces. Hope in every shard.

Our Purpose

Poured Into People

Femme Ferments exists to fund opportunity. Every bottle sold sends its profit directly to nonprofit partners working to expand opportunity and advance equity for women in our region.

We partner with local growers and producers who share our values, because doing right by the land and doing right by our community were never separate goals — they're the same harvest.

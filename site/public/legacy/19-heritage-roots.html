Our Origin

From This Soil

“Our wines are born from the generosity of this land and its people.”

Deep in the Willamette Valley, volcanic soil and generous rain give root to some of the world's most expressive Pinot Noir and Albariño. We partner with growers who have tended this earth for generations, trading fair value for fruit and stewardship for soil. This is not our land — we are only borrowing its gifts, gratefully, one harvest at a time.

Plant Your Roots

“The strongest vines grow together.”

Plant

Harvest volunteer, grape picking at partner vineyards. Get your hands in the soil where it all begins.

🌿

Tend

Help in the cellar — bottling, labeling, winemaking. Tend the fruit through every stage of transformation.

🌸

Bloom

Creative work: photography, design, brand storytelling. Help the story bloom in ways words alone can't.

🌳

Shelter

Host events, tastings, and community gatherings. Give the roots a place to grow above ground.

🌾

Cultivate

Tech, operations, building the infrastructure. Cultivate the systems that let everything else grow.

🍂

Seed

Connect wineries, nonprofits, and sponsors — spread the word. Scatter the seeds that start the next harvest.

Who We Are

This is bigger than wine.

“We're a community of volunteers, dreamers, and winemakers who believe every woman deserves a seat at the table.”

The wine is what brings us together — but the reason we keep showing up is each other. Every bottle represents dozens of hands, hundreds of volunteer hours, and one shared belief: our community shows up for one another, every single time.

There are as many ways to belong to this collective as there are pieces of glass. Find the one that fits your hands.

Vineyard & Harvest

Harvest Volunteer

Help pick donated grapes at partner vineyards across the Willamette Valley during harvest season. No experience required — just steady hands and an early alarm. It's dawn light, cold fingers, and the first step in every bottle we make.

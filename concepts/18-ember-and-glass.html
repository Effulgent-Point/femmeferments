The Flame

Ignited by Purpose

100%
Of Our Profits Empower Women

Femme Ferments began as a spark — a belief that wine could do more than fill a glass. Every bottle we release is fueled by donated grapes, volunteer hands, and cellar space lent by neighbors who believe in the same fire we do. Because so much is given freely, everything we earn can be given away in full, funding nonprofit partners who expand opportunity and advance equity for women in our community.

Our Vision

EveryPieceMatters

Strength in the fractures.
Grace in the pieces.
Hope in every shard.

Femme Ferments is a nonprofit wine collective in the Willamette Valley. We craft small-batch bottles so that the people around us — growers, volunteers, hosts, drinkers — can fund the work of empowering women. No piece of this is small. No piece stands alone.

No single tile makes the picture. Find the piece that fits you — the hours, the skills, the connections you have to give.

How It Works

The Process

I.

Donated Grapes

Partner Willamette Valley wineries donate fruit each harvest, so every vintage begins as a gift.

II.

Crafted Wine

Volunteers craft each vintage by hand — donated corks and repurposed screw‑tops keep costs at zero.

III.

100% Impact

Every dollar of profit flows straight to our nonprofit partners. No exceptions, no overhead skimmed.

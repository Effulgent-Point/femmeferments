Wines Crafted for a Purpose

Printed in the Willamette Valley — Est. this Vintage

Every bottle we produce begins with a broken thing made beautiful again. Femme Ferments was founded on the belief that craft — patient, deliberate, hand-tended craft — can carry more than flavor. It can carry purpose.

Nothing about our wine is manufactured in the ordinary sense. It is assembled, vintage by vintage, from the generosity of an entire valley: growers who donate their fruit, volunteers who donate their hands, and winemakers who donate their craft.

“We didn't set out to own a vineyard. We set out to prove that a whole valley could pour itself into something greater.”

Grapes are picked by volunteer harvest crews at partner vineyards, corks and glass are contributed by valley suppliers, and each lot is finished at a partner winery under the guidance of women winemakers. Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is mass-produced.

And when the glass is empty, the work continues: one hundred percent of our profits are donated directly to organizations that empower women across Oregon — because a bottle shared should leave the world a little better than it found it.

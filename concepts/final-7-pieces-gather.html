A Vintner's Note — Est. This Vintage

Every label starts somewhere, and ours starts with something broken. Femme Ferments began with a simple, stubborn belief: that the pieces of a shattered glass could be gathered up and made into something more beautiful than the glass had ever been whole.

We are a nonprofit wine label built on donated fruit, donated hours, and the quiet generosity of an entire valley. Growers give us the grapes the market didn't need. Volunteers give us their weekends. Women winemakers give us their craft, guiding every lot from crush to cork.

“We didn't set out to own a vineyard. We set out to prove that an entire valley could pour itself into something greater.”

Nothing about this label is manufactured in the ordinary sense — it is assembled, vintage by vintage, one favor and one afternoon at a time, from growers, harvesters, and winemakers who believe the work is worth doing.

And when the glass is empty, the story keeps going: one hundred percent of our profits are donated directly to programs that empower women across Oregon.

Where Every Bottle Comes From
Donated Grapes +

Each vintage begins with fruit that would otherwise go unsold — surplus and end-of-row grapes donated by growers across the Eola-Amity Hills, Dundee Hills, and the broader Willamette Valley appellations. What the market doesn't need, our winemakers turn into something extraordinary.

Corks & Glass +

Bottles, corks, capsules, and labels are contributed by valley suppliers who believe in what we're building. Nothing is purchased at full price — every material carries someone else's generosity before it ever reaches your table.

Volunteer Labor +

From harvest crews to bottling lines, every hour of labor behind Femme Ferments is volunteered. Women and allies across the Valley give their weekends, their hands, and their expertise so the proceeds can go further.

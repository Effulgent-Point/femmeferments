Our Story

Every Bottle Begins With a Break

What if every bottle told a story of resilience?

Femme Ferments started with a simple, stubborn belief: that something broken doesn’t need to be hidden or fixed — it can be rebuilt into something more beautiful than it was before. In the Willamette Valley, where the soil and the seasons ask everything of a vine, we found the same truth in the women we work alongside.

We are a nonprofit wine collective. Growers donate fruit, volunteers donate their hands, and partner wineries lend us their cellars — and every bottle that results carries that generosity forward, one glass at a time.

Like the shattered glass in our own mark, the pieces of this project came from many different places. Assembled together, they make something whole.

Our Mission
0%
Of Our Profits Empower Women

Every bottle we sell exists because someone gave something away — donated grapes, donated hours, donated cellar space. Because so much of what we need is given freely, all of the profit from every bottle can go straight back out: funding grants for housing stability, job training, and small-business support for women rebuilding their lives. Nothing is held back for ourselves. That’s not a marketing line — it’s the entire reason this collective exists.

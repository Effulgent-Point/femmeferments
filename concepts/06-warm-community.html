What We Stand For

A community built on warmth

Every bottle we pour is a chance to gather, give, and grow together.

Collaborate

We partner with women winemakers and growers across the valley, pooling talent to craft something bigger than any one of us.

Support

Every glass poured helps fund grants, mentorship, and resources for women building their futures in wine and beyond.

The Cause

Every bottle builds a bridge

Femme Ferments began with a simple idea: wine has always brought people to the table, so let it bring opportunity too. We partner with women-led vineyards, hire women across our supply chain, and reinvest every dollar of profit back into programs that help women thrive.

From scholarship funds to small business grants, your glass of wine becomes someone else's next chapter.

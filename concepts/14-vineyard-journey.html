Beauty in the Broken Glass

What if every bottle could change a life?

Femme Ferments is a nonprofit wine project rooted in Oregon’s Willamette Valley. We craft small-batch wines the way we craft change — imperfectly, collaboratively, with everyone’s hands on the bottle. This is the story of how a bottle travels from vine to glass to gift, and where you fit into it.

I
Chapter One

The Vineyard

It starts with a gift.

Every vintage begins with grapes we didn’t grow ourselves — donated by generous partner vineyards across the Willamette Valley who believe in what we’re building. No purchase orders, no invoices. Just growers who say yes when we ask. Each cluster carries someone’s generosity before it ever carries flavor.

II
Chapter Two

The Cellar

Crafted by many hands.

Our wine isn’t made by one winemaker working alone — it’s made by a rotating crew of volunteers who show up to punch down, rack, and taste. The corks are donated. The cellar space is borrowed. What we lack in budget, we make up for in the number of hands willing to help. Every bottle is proof that scarcity and generosity can share a room.

Get Involved Join the Makers →

Help in the cellar, bottling, and labeling — the most hands-on way to be part of every vintage.

III
Chapter Three

The Label

Every bottle tells a story.

The mosaic of broken glass on our label isn’t decoration — it’s the whole philosophy. We chose shattered glass because broken things, rearranged with care, become something more beautiful than they were whole. That’s the label. That’s the wine. That’s the women we support.

Chapter Six

The Impact

100%
Of Our Profits Empower Women

No overhead. No shareholders. Every dollar that isn’t reinvested in the next vintage goes straight to nonprofit partners advancing opportunity and equity for women. It works because almost everyone involved volunteers their time, and almost everything we use — grapes, corks, cellar space — is given, not bought.

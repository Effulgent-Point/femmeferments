Femme Ferments

Femme Ferments donates 100% of profits to nonprofit partners advancing opportunity and equity for women. Rooted in Oregon's Willamette Valley, we partner with local growers and producers, honoring craftsmanship, integrity, and community engagement in every bottle we release. Partner organizations will be announced as our first vintages come to market.

Component Concepts

Community Cards

Eight ways to present the same six roles — volunteer, winemaker, dreamer, organizer, advocate, and partner — each represented by a piece of our shattered-glass mosaic instead of an icon or photo. Scroll through and pick a favorite.

Variation 1

Glass Gallery

Large centered pieces on a parchment card with a thin gold frame — a clean, gallery-style grid that lets each shard of glass be the hero.

The Volunteer

Shows up before dawn at harvest — no experience required, just willing hands.

The Winemaker

Guides every donated grape from vine to bottle in cellars across the valley.

The Dreamer

Holds the vision that turned a wine label into a movement for women.

The Organizer

Turns a bottle of wine into a room full of people who believe the same thing.

The Advocate

Speaks up for the mission and connects us to partners who share it.

The Partner

Donates grapes, cellar space, and equipment — the backbone of every vintage.
